## Basic Troubleshooting Facts

- **Furnace won't start**: check that the thermostat is set to "heat" and the batteries aren't dead; check that the furnace filter isn't visibly clogged (a clogged filter can trigger a safety shutoff).
- **AC blowing warm air**: check the outdoor unit isn't visibly iced over; if it is, turn the system off and let it thaw for a few hours before calling.
- **No hot water**: check the water heater's pilot light (gas units) or breaker (electric units) before assuming a technician visit is needed.
- **Low water pressure**: often caused by a partially closed shutoff valve or a clogged aerator, not always a major plumbing issue.
- None of the above replaces a professional diagnosis — if a fix isn't obvious or safe to attempt, we recommend booking a visit rather than continuing to troubleshoot.
