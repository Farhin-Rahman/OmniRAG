# Platform Trust & Safety Policy

Reference policy written for this project, modeled on the published trust-and-safety practices of major crowdfunding platforms. It is the policy the risk-assessment step retrieves from and cites.

## Campaign Vetting Process

The platform uses a multi-layered approval process before a campaign goes live:

- **Sanctions screening**: every account is screened against international sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering enforcement lists.
- **Documentation review**: the platform requests supporting materials to verify the fundraising cause, and connected accounts must show a traceable path for funds to reach the final beneficiary.
- **Enhanced due diligence**: campaigns raising funds outside G7 countries, particularly in sanctioned jurisdictions, undergo additional verification of all associated parties.
- **Manual pre-launch vetting**: every campaign is reviewed by a person to confirm it follows the platform's guidelines before it is published.

## Prohibited Content

Campaigns may not incite hate or violence towards any other party.

## Fraud Detection & Monitoring

The platform runs regular checks on higher-risk transactions — specialists review donations to identify fraudulent behavior and, when a transaction looks suspicious, contact the donor to confirm it.

## Enforcement

When fraud is discovered, the platform permanently bars the flagged user and refunds all funds they raised back to the donors.
