# LaunchGood Trust & Safety Policy

Source: LaunchGood's public Safety & Compliance page (launchgood.com/safety), retrieved September 2026. Quoted phrases are LaunchGood's own published language.

## Campaign Vetting Process

LaunchGood uses a multi-layered approval system before a campaign goes live:

- **Sanctions screening**: every account is screened against over 400 sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering enforcement lists.
- **Documentation review**: the platform requests supporting materials to verify the fundraising cause, and connected accounts must show "a clear path of funds distribution to the final beneficiary."
- **Enhanced due diligence**: campaigns raising funds outside G7 countries, particularly in OFAC-sanctioned jurisdictions, undergo additional verification of all associated parties.
- **Manual pre-launch vetting**: "every campaign is manually reviewed by our team to make sure it follows our website guidelines."

## Prohibited Content

Campaigns may not incite hate or violence towards any other party.

## Fraud Detection & Monitoring

LaunchGood runs "regular checks on risk transactions" — specialists review donations to identify fraudulent behavior and, when a transaction looks suspicious, contact the donor to confirm it.

## Enforcement

When fraud is discovered, LaunchGood blacklists the flagged user from using the platform again and refunds all funds they raised back to the donors.
